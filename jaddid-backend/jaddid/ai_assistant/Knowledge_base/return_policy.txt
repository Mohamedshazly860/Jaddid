Return Policy

Overview

The store accepts eligible product returns according to the conditions described in this policy. Return eligibility depends on the product, its condition, the time since delivery, and the reason for the return.

Return Eligibility

A product may be eligible for return when it meets the store's return requirements. Customers should request a return as soon as possible after receiving the order.

Returned products should generally be in an acceptable condition and include the original accessories, packaging, and other items that were included with the order when applicable.

Products that have been significantly used, damaged by the customer, modified, or returned without required components may not qualify for a return.

Some product categories may have additional return restrictions.

Return Request

Customers who want to return a product should contact the store through the available return or support process and provide the relevant order information.

The return request may require:

* Order information
* Product information
* Reason for the return
* Photos or other evidence when the product is damaged or defective

The store may review the request before approving the return.

Damaged or Defective Products

If a product arrives damaged or has a manufacturing defect, the customer should report the issue as soon as possible.

The customer may be asked to provide photos, videos, or other information that helps the store evaluate the problem.

If the issue is confirmed, the store may offer an appropriate resolution according to the applicable return, replacement, refund, or warranty terms.

Incorrect Products

If the customer receives a product that is different from the item ordered, the customer should contact support and provide the order details.

The store will review the case and determine the appropriate resolution.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, the refund process depends on the payment method and the store's applicable procedures.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear may vary depending on the payment provider or financial institution.

Shipping and Return Costs

Return shipping costs may depend on the reason for the return and the applicable store policy.

For example, cases involving an incorrect, damaged, or defective product may be handled differently from a return based solely on customer preference.

Non-Returnable Products

Certain products may be excluded from returns because of their nature, condition, customization, hygiene requirements, or other applicable restrictions.

Customers should check the product-specific return information when available.

Important Limitations

This document provides general return information. Product-specific conditions, checkout information, and the store's current return procedures may take precedence.

The chatbot must not guarantee that a specific return will be approved unless the available store information explicitly supports that conclusion.

If the required information is unavailable, the customer should be directed to the store's support process for confirmation.
