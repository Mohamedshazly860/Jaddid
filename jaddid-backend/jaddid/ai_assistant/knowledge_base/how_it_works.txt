How It Works

Overview

The website provides an online marketplace where customers can browse available products, discover products that match their needs, and place orders through the application.

Browsing Products

Customers can browse the product catalog and explore products by category or other available filters. Each product may contain information such as its name, description, price, category, characteristics, and availability.

The information displayed for a product is based on the data maintained by the application.

Searching for Products

Customers can search for products using the website's search functionality. Search criteria may include product names, categories, brands, prices, or other product attributes supported by the application.

Customers can also describe what they are looking for to the AI assistant. The assistant can help identify relevant products based on the available catalog information.

Product Recommendations

The AI assistant can help customers narrow down product choices by considering requirements such as intended use, budget, preferred characteristics, or other relevant criteria.

Recommendations are based on the information available to the assistant. The assistant should not claim that a product has a feature or characteristic when that information is not available in the product data.

Viewing Product Details

Customers can open a product to view its available details before making a purchase decision.

Product information may include price, description, category, available characteristics, and inventory status. The exact information depends on the product and the application's data.

Adding Products to the Cart

When a customer decides to purchase a product, the product can be added to the shopping cart through the website.

The customer can review the selected products and quantities before proceeding to checkout.

Placing an Order

During checkout, the customer provides the information required to complete the order. This may include delivery information and other details required by the application.

Before confirming the order, customers should review the selected products, quantities, delivery information, and applicable costs.

Order Processing and Delivery

After an order is confirmed, the store processes the order and prepares it for delivery.

Customers may be able to track their order depending on the tracking functionality available in the application.

Delivery times can vary based on location, product availability, processing time, and shipping conditions.

After Delivery

Customers should check the delivered products and contact the store if they receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item.

Eligible products may be returned according to the Return Policy.

Customer Support

If a customer cannot find the required information or encounters a problem with an order, they should use the support channels provided by the store.

The AI assistant can provide information available in the store's knowledge base and product catalog, but it should not invent information that is unavailable.

AI Assistant

The AI assistant can help with product discovery, recommendations, product information, shipping questions, return-policy questions, and other supported store-related topics.

For questions outside the store's supported domain, the assistant should politely explain that it is designed to help with the store and its services rather than general-purpose questions.
